Different Rank, Different Work

The Badge Changes The Diagnosis. Not The Process.

Open the rank range closest to you. These are common coaching priorities, not guaranteed outcomes or promised timelines.

Iron through Gold: what usually gets reviewed?

Fundamentals, mechanics, movement, fight selection, agent consistency, basic decision patterns, and practice quality often have the highest leverage. Your exact priority still comes from your gameplay.

Platinum through Diamond: what usually changes?

The obvious fundamentals may already be understood, so the work becomes more about repeatability, decision quality, positioning, timing, role execution, and identifying which inconsistency is actually deciding rounds.

Ascendant through Radiant: is coaching too basic?

No. Higher level mistakes are often smaller, more contextual, and harder to self diagnose. The process stays the same: watch the real game, find the highest value pattern, build the correction, and verify it.
