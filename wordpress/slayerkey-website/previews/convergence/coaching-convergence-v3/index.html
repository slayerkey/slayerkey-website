Different Rank, Different Work

The Higher You Climb, The Smaller The Mistakes Get.

Iron through Gold

Platinum through Diamond

The work becomes more about repeatability, decision quality, positioning, timing, role execution, and identifying which inconsistency is deciding rounds.

Ascendant through Radiant

Higher level mistakes are often smaller and more contextual, which makes outside diagnosis more useful, not less.
