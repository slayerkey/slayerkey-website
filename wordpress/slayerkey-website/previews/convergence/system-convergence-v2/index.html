You can find ten mistakes in your own VOD and still have no idea which one deserves the next week of practice. You can copy a new aim routine and still have no idea whether mechanics are your actual bottleneck.

The problem is not that you have learned too little. It is that every bad game creates a new theory about what you should fix.
