Questions Before You Decide

Know Exactly What You're Choosing.

Is this for my rank?

Yes. The mentorship is built on diagnosing your specific gameplay, so it works whether you are climbing out of Silver or grinding Immortal. The mistakes change with rank. The process of finding them does not. The higher you go, the smaller and harder to self diagnose the mistakes get, which is exactly when an outside set of eyes matters most.

What happens right after I pay?

You get a confirmation with your next steps immediately: how to book your first session with Slayerkey and how to get access to the Improvement System and the Training Dojo. Your first session is where the full diagnosis starts.

How much access do I get between sessions?

Two private sessions each month, personalized VOD review between them, and priority support for questions while you apply the work. The point of the structure is that corrections happen while the habit is still fixable, not weeks later.

What if my problem is mechanics? Or decisions? Or consistency?

That is exactly what the first diagnosis answers. Most players guess wrong about their own biggest problem, and then train the wrong thing for months. You do not need to know what is wrong before you start. Finding that out is the job.

Should I join the Training Dojo instead?

The Dojo is genuinely good, and for many players it is the right choice. The difference is personal involvement. In the Dojo you improve with structure, coaching, and community. In the mentorship, I am personally in your game for four months: your VODs, your habits, your priorities, your corrections. If you want to keep diagnosing your own game, join the Dojo. If you want me to do it, this is the tier where that happens.

What is the schedule commitment?

Sessions are scheduled around mutual availability, two per month. If life gets busy, one pause of up to three weeks is available during the mentorship. Cancellations with less than two hours notice count as used sessions, because that time was reserved for you.

Is the payment plan a subscription I can cancel anytime?

No. The mentorship is one fixed four month engagement. The payment plan is the same purchase split into four monthly payments of $325, and stopping participation does not cancel the remaining payments. If you are not sure you can commit to four months, do not start yet. The full details are in the Terms & Conditions.

Do you guarantee a rank?

No, and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. Your improvement depends on you actually playing and applying the work. What I can tell you is what the process delivers: you will know exactly what is holding your game back, why, and what to do about it, checked and corrected month after month. The results players have gotten from that are on this page.

Is this better than just watching your videos?

The videos teach concepts that apply to everyone. The mentorship is about the mistakes that are specific to you. If watching videos was going to fix your game, four months of direct diagnosis is not what you need. If you have been watching them and your rank still does not match what you know, that is the exact problem this exists to solve.
