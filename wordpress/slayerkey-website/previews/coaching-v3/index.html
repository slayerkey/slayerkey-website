Questions Before You Book

Know Exactly What You Are Choosing.

Should I book a Single Session or Performance Mentorship?

Book the Single Session if you mainly want an expert diagnosis of your current game and a clear plan for what to work on next. Choose Performance Mentorship if you want Slayerkey involved across the full 90 day improvement process with repeated sessions, VOD reviews, a custom roadmap, playbook, and direct voice note access.

Can I start with one session and upgrade later?

Yes. The Single Session includes credit toward Performance Mentorship if you upgrade within 14 days.

What actually gets reviewed?

Your real gameplay. The focus is on the decisions, rounds, mistakes, and recurring patterns that are most responsible for your current results, then turning those into clear priorities you can apply.

What changes with Performance Mentorship after the first session?

The process can repeat across six private sessions over 90 days. That gives Slayerkey the chance to see what happens after the first correction, review new gameplay, and keep adjusting the roadmap as the next bottleneck becomes clear.

How is Coaching different from the Training Dojo or Improvement System?

Coaching is the highest touch option and the fastest route to personal diagnosis when you want direct expert attention. The Dojo is ongoing training, feedback, community, accountability, and progression. The Improvement System is a self directed one time purchase with lifetime structure and resources.
