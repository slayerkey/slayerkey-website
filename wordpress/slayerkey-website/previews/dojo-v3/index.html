Questions Before You Join

Know Exactly What You're Buying.

Is this for my rank?

Yes. The process is built around finding the next problem in your games, regardless of where you are starting. The priority changes with the player; the Diagnose → Prescribe → Train → Apply → Review loop does not.

Is this just another Discord server?

No. Discord is where much of the coaching and community interaction lives. The product is the structured improvement system around your 7 Day Improvement Plan, Monthly Improvement Roadmap, Tracker reviews, coaching, feedback, training, practice, and accountability.

Will Slayerkey actually review my gameplay and help me personally?

Yes. Tracker reviews, weekly coaching, and daily feedback are available from the start. Personal Mechanics Analysis and Personal VOD Review are higher touch progression milestones for Monthly members. Annual members get both of those personal reviews immediately.

What happens after I join?

You start with the 7 Day Improvement Plan so you know what to do first. From there the Monthly Improvement Roadmap moves through focused improvement themes while you keep getting Tracker feedback, coaching, daily help, and the training that matches what you are working on.

What is the difference between Monthly and Annual?

Both plans include the complete Dojo. Monthly is $19.99 per month and unlocks the higher touch personal reviews as you progress. Annual is $199.99 per year, saves two months, and gives you both the Personal Mechanics Analysis and Personal VOD Review immediately, plus the custom routine and resources from your mechanics analysis.

When do I get the Personal Mechanics Analysis and Personal VOD Review?

Monthly members receive them as progression milestones. The Personal Mechanics Analysis unlocks around Month 2, and the Personal VOD Review later, around Month 4. Annual members get both immediately. Your Tracker Review, weekly coaching, and daily feedback are available from the start on either plan.

Why would I buy this instead of watching free YouTube videos?

Free videos are useful for learning concepts. They cannot diagnose which mistake is costing you RR right now, tell you which skill deserves priority, watch whether you are applying the fix correctly, or adjust the plan based on your next game. That feedback loop is the difference.

How does the 400 RR guarantee work?

The guarantee is meant for players who genuinely use the system. Complete the core improvement work, play at least 50 ranked games during the 90 day period, participate in the Dojo, and if you still do not gain the promised 400 RR, ask for a refund. The point is simple: genuinely use the system and do the work, and the guarantee is there to protect you.

How is the Dojo different from the Improvement System or 1:1 Coaching?

The Dojo is ongoing structure, feedback, community, training, and accountability. The Improvement System is a self directed one time purchase with a lifetime framework. 1:1 Coaching is the highest touch direct personal help.
