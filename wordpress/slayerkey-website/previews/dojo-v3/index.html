Questions Before You Join

Know Exactly What You're Getting.

Is this for my rank?

Yes. Whether you're trying to escape Silver or make the jump into Immortal, the roadmap is built around mastering the same foundation: fundamentals, mechanics, maps, your agent, and a repeatable improvement process. The specific mistakes change as you climb. The system for getting better does not.

Is this just another Discord server?

No. Discord is where the community, Weekly Group Coaching, Daily Q&A, Premier teams, challenges, and discussion happen. The product is the 90-Day Improvement Roadmap and the improvement system built around it. You are joining with a clear Day 1, a clear Day 30, and a clear path through Day 90.

What happens after I join?

Start with the 7-Day Fundamentals phase. You'll establish your improvement system and then move through Mechanics → Map Mastery → Agent Mastery. Instead of wondering which of the 100+ lessons to watch, the roadmap tells you what deserves your attention next.

When do I get my Mechanics Review?

Your Mechanics Review is the Day 30 milestone of the roadmap. Monthly members unlock it after their first 30 days. Annual members unlock it immediately. You'll find the biggest weakness holding back your mechanics and leave with a clear routine for what to fix next.

What's the difference between Monthly and Annual?

Both include the complete Training Dojo. Monthly is $19.99 per month and includes the Mechanics Review at Day 30. Annual is $199.99 per year, saves two months, and unlocks your Day 30 Mechanics Review immediately.

Why would I buy this instead of watching free YouTube videos?

YouTube is great at giving you more things you could work on. That's also the problem. One video says aim train. Another says movement. Another says game sense. Another gives you ten tricks for an agent you might stop playing next week. The Dojo puts everything into a clear order. You know what to train now, what comes next, and where to find the exact resources when you need them.

How does the 400 RR guarantee work?

Finish the complete 90-Day Improvement Roadmap. If you follow the roadmap and do not gain 400 RR during those 90 days, you get your money back.

How is the Dojo different from the Improvement System or Private Coaching?

Training Dojo: follow the roadmap alongside a serious community with Weekly Group Coaching, Daily Q&A, Premier teams, challenges, ongoing resources, and your Mechanics Review. Improvement System: a self-directed one-time purchase for players who want the framework without the ongoing community. Private Coaching: direct personal coaching for players who want Slayerkey personally diagnosing their gameplay, reviewing their games, and helping decide exactly what they should work on.
